# A. P. J. Abdul Kalam Tribute

A responsive tribute page about Indian aerospace scientist and former President A. P. J. Abdul Kalam. Biography and milestone facts are paraphrased from the President of India website. The portrait is hosted on Wikimedia Commons and marked CC0 (public-domain dedication); its credit and source are shown on the page.

Open `index.html` in a browser with internet access to load the remote image and web fonts.
